OPERATIONAL CONTROLS

Tolerance Settings

Set warning thresholds for sales and tank differences.

SALES VALIDATION

Sales Difference Tolerance

Maximum sales difference allowed at handover.

Example: 1.00 allows a difference of up to ETB 1.00. This value is used for attendant submission and backend validation.

TANK RECONCILIATION

Shift Handover Tank Difference Tolerance

Allowed stock difference at handover, in liters.

Default: 10 L. A larger difference does not block the handover; it triggers an admin notification so the tank reading can be checked.

PURCHASE VALIDATION

Purchase Discharge Tank Difference Tolerance

Maximum allowed difference, in liters, between system stock and the physical tank reading before purchase discharge.

Default: 10 L. A larger difference does not block the discharge; it triggers an admin notification so the physical stock can be checked.
